## Slide 2: The Urban Renewable Paradox (Problem)
- **Land Scarcity & Aesthetic Barriers:** Dense European urban and industrial sites face zero-carbon ESG mandates but lack land/roof space.
- **Rooftop Solar Limits:** Requires ~300 m² horizontal area for 45 kW peak, inactive at night and during heavy cloud cover.
- **Conventional Wind Limits:** Low-frequency noise (>45 dB), flicker effect, and poor performance in turbulent urban airflow.

---

## Slide 3: The Treetino Breakthrough (Solution & Core Tech)
- **Dual-Modality Biomimetic Architecture:** 12 m vertical tree structure combining 12 ducted VAWT rotors + 300 heliotropic solar leaves.
- **FZU Aerodynamic Stator Ducting:** Proprietary transparent shroud accelerates turbulent urban wind into rotor buckets (**+27% efficiency boost**, cut-in wind speed **2.0 m/s**).
- **Acoustic & Visual Elimination:** Noise **< 35 dB(A) @ 10 m**, zero shadow flickering.
- **Heliotropic AI Tracking:** **+32% solar yield boost** over fixed rooftop PV.

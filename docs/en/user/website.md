# Website and language

## Public pages

The website can be used without an account. Its main public areas are:

- the home page;
- product pages for Treetino V1, Treetino V2, and the wind turbine;
- the product configurator;
- collaboration information;
- media;
- contact.

Use the main navigation or page calls to action to move between these areas.

`[[SCREENSHOT]] - Main navigation on the Treetino home page`

## Product pages

Each product page presents the corresponding product and links into its configuration flow:

- Treetino V1: `/products/treetino-v1`
- Treetino V2: `/products/treetino-v2`
- Wind turbine: `/products/turbine`

The configurator can also be opened directly with a product already selected.

`[[SCREENSHOT]] - Product overview with the configurator call to action`

## Changing the language

The website supports Czech and English. Select the language in the language switcher. The choice is saved in the browser and is reused on later visits.

If there is no saved choice, the site uses Czech by default and English as its fallback language.

## Cookie preferences

The cookie panel distinguishes necessary cookies from analytics cookies. Necessary cookies are always enabled because the application needs them to operate. Analytics consent is optional.

`[[SCREENSHOT]] - Cookie consent panel with necessary and analytics choices`

## Contacting Treetino

Open `/contact` and enter:

1. your name;
2. a valid email address;
3. your message.

Submit the form. A successful response confirms that the message was stored. If the email belongs to an existing customer, the message is associated with that customer account; otherwise it is stored as a guest message.

`[[SCREENSHOT]] - Completed contact form before submission`

All three fields are required. Correct the highlighted field if the form reports a missing name, invalid email, or missing message.

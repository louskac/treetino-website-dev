### SCENE 2: The Technological Breakthrough & TRL 5 Proof (0:35 – 1:20)
- **Visual:** Close-up engineering footage of the transparent Venturi stator shroud wrapping the wind rotors, airflow visualization, solar leaf heliotropic movement, and CTU power electronics integration.
- **Presenter:** **Jakub Lustyk (CTO)** inside the testing control station and next to the wind rotor shroud.
- **Script (Jakub):**
  > "Our core breakthrough lies in our proprietary transparent ducted wind turbine, co-developed with the Institute of Physics of the Czech Academy of Sciences. In turbulent urban environments, standard vertical turbines suffer severe efficiency loss. Our transparent Venturi shrouds accelerate incoming airflow into the rotor buckets, delivering a measured **+27% efficiency boost** and starting rotation at just 2.0 m/s wind speed—all while operating below 35 decibels.
  > 
  > Above the turbines, 300 PV leaves dynamically track the sun using our AI algorithms, increasing solar yield by **+32%**. We have already validated this complete integrated system over 6 months of outdoor testing, generating over 24,000 kWh on a single 1:1 prototype."

## Production Checklist & Guidelines for EIC Compliance
1. **Presenters:** Must feature **3 key team members** (Dominik, Jakub, Monika) actively speaking on camera.
2. **Real Physical Hardware:** 100% of footage must show real physical prototype hardware, wind shrouds, solar leaf tracking actuators, and manufacturing assembly—**zero generic stock footage**.
3. **Audio Quality:** Professional wireless lavalier microphones to ensure crisp, studio-quality speech over background wind noise.
4. **Data Accuracy:** All spoken numbers (45 kW, 1.2 m², €235k, +27% wind boost, +32% solar boost, €705k contract) must strictly match the EIC Short Proposal Part B.
